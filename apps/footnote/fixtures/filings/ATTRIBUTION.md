## Source check

Every table and figure on the kept pages was checked against its "Data source", "Source" and "Note" lines. The overview table (page 1), the forecast-changes table (page 3), Table 1 (page 4) and the Brent price chart (page 5) each give "Data source: U.S. Energy Information Administration, Short-Term Energy Outlook". Page 4's narrative text also mentions ship-tracking data from a commercial provider; no table or figure reproduces that data.
